01. About me

I'm a full-stack developer and AI engineer with a Master of Science in Computer Science. By day I'm the Senior Information System Analyst for the Department of Health's Philippine Multisectoral Nutrition Project (PMNP), where I own the technical side of PMNP-IS — a DHIS2-based national information system used by community volunteers, supervisors and analysts to track the nutrition of mothers and children across hundreds of barangays.

Alongside that, I work as a contract developer for Numat Group in New Zealand, shipping production tools for their agri-shed, health-and-safety and site-visit workflows — React front-ends, Supabase/Postgres back-ends, and integrations with NetSuite, HubSpot and SharePoint.

I like problems that sit between data, people and operations: making a field app work offline in a remote barangay, tuning a Postgres analytics query from minutes to milliseconds, or teaching an AI agent to read email, drive a browser and finish the task itself. I lean heavily on AI-augmented development to move fast — and on tests, probes and proofs to make sure fast is also correct.

02. Experience

Senior Information System Analyst

Department of Health (DOH) — Philippine Multisectoral Nutrition Project (PMNP) · Manila

  • Technical lead for PMNP-IS, a DHIS2 Tracker-based national nutrition information system covering household registration, quarterly scorecard interviews and program indicators.
  • Build and maintain the custom DHIS2 apps used in the field — the field-interviewer app and the supervisor app — including the household lifecycle/status workflow, roster management and offline-sync edge cases.
  • Implemented the nutrition scorecard engine: WHO growth-standard z-scores (WFA / WFH / WFL), immunization (FIC/CIC) and ANC/PNC compliance rules, and dietary-diversity logic.
  • Designed the Randomizer sampling tool (primary/reserve draws) and the household Masterlist reports used for monitoring and evaluation.
  • Own database and analytics performance on the live PostgreSQL/DHIS2 stack — diagnosing runaway analytics queries, adding missing partition indexes and fixing program-indicator SQL.
  • Run the support-ticket pipeline for field concerns, triaging and fixing live data issues, and authored the cloud-infrastructure (GCP) and data-privacy proposals for the system's sustainability.

DHIS2 · React · Node.js · PostgreSQL · Python · Google Cloud · Google Workspace APIs

Contract Full-Stack Developer

Numat Group · New Zealand (remote)

  • Agri Shed Costing Calculator — a production quoting tool for kitset sheds: wind-zone design loads, bay-by-bay geometry, roof pitch, cladding/pole/timber pricing tables, roller-door rules, BOM and Excel export. React + Supabase with row-level security.
  • Creo Site Visit System — capture-to-CRM workflow: site-visit capture with media, AI-generated NZ-English summaries, reviewer queue, SharePoint archiving and NetSuite sync via Microsoft Graph.

  • Supporting tooling: automated report generation API, company-data scraping/enrichment pipeline, and a youngstar product calculator.
